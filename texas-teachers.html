Ask Greg

Quick answers

Does the Social Security Fairness Act affect Texas teachers?

Yes. Most Texas school districts don't pay into Social Security, and two rules, the WEP and GPO, used to cut Social Security for teachers with a TRS pension. The law ended both for benefits payable from January 2024 on. If you never applied for a spousal or survivor benefit because of the GPO, you may need to apply now.

When can I retire with full TRS benefits?

At age 65 with at least five years of service, or when your age plus years of service equals 80. Tier 2 members must also be at least 60 for the Rule of 80, and Tier 3 members at least 62. Retiring earlier means a permanently reduced pension.

Does my TRS pension go up with inflation?

Not automatically. TRS has no built-in cost-of-living increase. Raises happen only when the Texas Legislature approves one, so your savings need to help keep up with rising prices.

Can I move my 403(b) while I'm still teaching?

Usually only after you leave the district, or at age 59 and a half if your plan allows it. Check for surrender charges before you move anything, and use a direct rollover to avoid 20% tax withholding.
