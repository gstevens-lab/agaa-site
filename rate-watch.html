How to compare guaranteed rates the honest way

  • Look at the yield over the whole term. Some products advertise a high first-year rate that drops after year one. The average you're guaranteed over the full term is the fair way to compare.
  • Simple vs. compound interest. Simple-interest products don't earn interest on interest, so their real yield is lower than the rate they advertise.
  • Check the minimum. The best rates often require $100,000 or more. Smaller amounts usually get a slightly lower rate.
  • Know the surrender schedule and any market value adjustment (MVA). If you take out more than the free amount early, you could pay a surrender charge, and an MVA can move the payout up or down with interest rates.
  • It's not a bank CD. A MYGA is an insurance contract, not FDIC insured. Its guarantee is backed by the insurance company, which is why I check every company's financial strength ratings before I show it to you.
