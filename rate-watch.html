How to read these rates

  • Look at the yield over the whole term. Some products advertise a high first-year rate that drops after year one. The average over the full term is the fair way to compare.
  • Simple vs. compound interest. Simple-interest products don't earn interest on interest, which is why their yield is lower than the rate they advertise.
  • Market value adjustment (MVA). If you take out more than the free amount early, the payout can be adjusted up or down with interest rates, on top of any surrender charge.
  • Not a bank CD. A MYGA is an insurance contract, not FDIC insured. Its guarantee is backed by the insurance company, which is why I check every company's ratings.
