What to do with your number

Most people plan to cover the gap by withdrawing from savings. That works, as long as the market cooperates and the money lasts as long as you do. The alternative is to cover the essential part of the gap with guaranteed income, so the bills get paid no matter what the market does.

On a call, I'll look at your gap, your savings and your timeline, and show you a few ways to close it, including options that don't involve an annuity.
