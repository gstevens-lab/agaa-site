CD coming due?

Before you renew, compare it to a MYGA.

A multi-year guaranteed annuity (MYGA) works a lot like a CD: a fixed rate, locked in for a set number of years. The differences, in rates and in taxes, can add up to real money.

Run the numbers Get today's rates from Greg

CD vs. MYGA at a glance

Bank CDMYGA
RateFixed for the termFixed for the term (often 2 to 10 years)
Taxes on interestTaxed every year, even if you don't touch itTax-deferred until you withdraw it
Backed byFDIC insurance, up to its limitsThe financial strength and claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company
Early accessEarly withdrawal penalty, usually a few months of interestMost allow about 10% a year free; more than that during the term carries a surrender charge
Before age 59½No IRS penaltyGains withdrawn before 59½ may face a 10% IRS penalty
At the end of the termRenew, cash out, or move itRenew, cash out, move it, or turn it into lifetime income

MYGAs fit money you won't need for the length of the term. Money you may need soon belongs somewhere more liquid.
