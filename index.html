Questions people usually ask me first

Not a sales pitch. Just honest answers to what's on people's minds.

Can I actually lose money in a fixed annuity?

Not from the market. A fixed or fixed indexed annuity protects your principal from market losses. That's the whole point of the product. What varies is how much it can grow. The things that can cost you are surrender charges if you take out more than the free amount early, and fees on optional riders. I'll show you all of them up front.

What's the catch with the bonus offers I keep hearing about?

Bonuses are real. They usually vest over several years, and bonus products often come with longer surrender periods or lower growth caps. I'll walk through the actual math with you, not just the headline number.

Do I have to move all of my money to talk to you?

No. Sometimes the right answer is to move part of it, sometimes none of it. A conversation costs you nothing and commits you to nothing.
