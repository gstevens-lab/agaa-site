This site is for general information only. It is not a recommendation to buy any specific product and is not tax or legal advice. Annuities are long-term insurance contracts. Features, rates and availability vary by carrier and state. Guarantees are backed by the financial strength and claims-paying ability of the issuing insurance company. Withdrawals above the contract's free amount during the surrender period may be subject to surrender charges, and withdrawals before age 59½ may be subject to a 10% IRS penalty.
